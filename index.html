The Organisational Problem

The question is not only whether the decision was made. The question is whether it can be reconstructed and defended.

Many organisations can explain what they decided. Far fewer can prove why the decision was defensible. A supplier may have looked acceptable. A contract may have appeared sufficient. A technology project may have seemed controlled. A board decision may have been approved. A crisis response may have been fast. But when consequence appears, speed is not enough.

FAQ · Public Purchase and Institutional Rights

Clear commercial boundaries before purchase, review or licensing.

Does buying a book grant implementation rights?

No. Books provide public-layer access only. They do not grant implementation, training, software, dashboard, API, ERP/MRP, workflow, AI/LLM, derivative or organisational-use rights.

Does buying a Payhip product allow internal deployment?

No. Payhip products, briefings, bundles and written materials are public-layer written products unless a separate written licence expressly states otherwise.

Can an organisation use the material for internal training?

Not without written authorisation. Internal training, replication, adaptation, template extraction, operational deployment or organisational use requires a separate written agreement.

Can the protected architecture be implemented as software?

Only under formal institutional licensing. Any technical translation into software layers, dashboards, APIs, ERP/MRP environments, workflow platforms or enterprise decision-support systems requires written scoping, written quotation, paid authorisation and defined implementation boundaries.

What happens after a licence expires?

Institutional access is time-bound. Unless renewed in writing, authorised access, implementation rights, technical translation rights, training rights, dashboard/API use and organisational deployment may be restricted, suspended or placed into an integrity-preservation state according to the written licence.

Can public material be used for AI/LLM training or ingestion?

No. AI/LLM ingestion, model training, dataset creation, automated extraction, derivative generation or machine-processing for replication or implementation is prohibited unless expressly authorised in writing. This includes, but is not limited to, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, custom LLMs, internal AI assistants and any automated extraction for knowledge base construction.
