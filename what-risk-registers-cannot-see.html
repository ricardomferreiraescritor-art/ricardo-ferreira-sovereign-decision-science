The Blind Spot

What the product exposes

A risk can be identified, scored, assigned and reviewed while the decision that created it remains structurally fragile because evidence, authority, dependency, optionality or corrective power are weak.

Audience

Who this is for

Risk, procurement, supply chain, finance, legal, audit, compliance, operations, technology, PMO and executive leadership.

What It Establishes

Executive reading outcomes

  • What risk registers do well—and where they stop.
  • The difference between recorded risk and decision robustness.
  • Why a named risk owner may still lack authority to correct the decision.
  • How fragility travels across functions and reaches the Board.
  • The public-layer logic behind Decision Fragility Index™ without formulas, weights, scoring or thresholds.

Executive Questions

Questions the decision must survive

  • How little must change before the decision stalls, reverses or collapses?
  • Does the named owner have the authority and resources to correct the exposure?
  • Which assumptions are carrying the decision without decision-grade evidence?
  • Would the decision remain defensible if one dependency, approval or recovery claim failed?
